          Meet Hermes

          What Hermes is, what it does, and what makes an agent different from a chatbot.

          Hermes Agent, by Nous Research

          “The Agent That Grows With You”

          Hermes is an AI agent you install once. It runs on your own computer (or in the cloud), works with almost any AI model, and gets more useful the more you use it: it remembers your projects and saves what worked as reusable skills.

          • Free and open source (MIT license). You only pay for the AI model, if you pick a paid one.
          • Yours: your chats, memory, and skills are saved in a folder on your computer.
          • Any model: Nous Portal, OpenRouter, OpenAI, or one running on your own PC.
          Visit the official site GitHub Docs

          What Hermes does

          The six features on the official site, in plain words, and where you try each one today.

          #1 ConnectLives EverywhereTalk to the same agent on Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, Signal, email, or the terminal. One agent, one memory, everywhere.
          #2 RememberPersistent MemoryIt remembers your projects, turns what worked into skills, and doesn’t forget how it solved a problem.
          #3 ScheduleFocused AutomationSay it in plain words, like “every morning at 8, send me a briefing”, and it runs on its own.
          #4 DelegateTasks MultipliedA big job can be split among helper agents (subagents) that work at the same time.
          #5 SearchBrowse the WebIt can search the web, use a browser, look at images, make images, and talk out loud.
          #6 ExperimentIsolated SandboxingIts commands can run in a safe, separate box (Docker, SSH, or the cloud) instead of straight on your computer.

          Three ways to run it

          Desktop appFor macOS 12+ and Windows 10/11 (Microsoft Store). The easiest start.
          TerminalOne command on macOS, Linux, or Windows. This is what you’ll learn in step 2.
          In the cloudAn always-on agent hosted for you, through Nous Portal. Optional and paid.

          Questions people ask

          How much does it cost?

          Hermes itself is free and open source (MIT license). The AI model is separate: use your own provider, or Nous Portal, which has a free tier with free models and paid plans for more. Cloud hosting is optional.

          How do I get started?

          Install the desktop app (macOS or Windows) or run the install command, connect a model provider in the setup, then start chatting. Step 2 walks you through it.

          How do I chat with my agent?

          In the desktop app or the terminal, or connect a messaging app like Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, Signal, or email. You’ll connect Telegram in step 11.

          Does it remember things between sessions?

          Yes. Hermes saves your conversations, memories, and skills, so you can pick up later. Back up your Hermes folder (~/.hermes) if you move to another computer.

          What happens when I stop it?

          It stops running, but nothing is deleted: its chats, memories, and skills stay. Start it again to continue. Scheduled jobs don’t run while it’s stopped.

              Skills: saved how-tos

hermes skills list              # what you have
              
  
              
  
              Write your own skill
              
  
              ~/.hermes/skills/study-plan/SKILL.md
              ---
name: study-plan
description: Turn a list of exams into a week-by-week study plan.
version: 1.0.0
---

# Study plan

## When to Use
The user lists exams or deadlines and asks for a plan.

## Procedure
1. Ask for each subject, its date, and free hours per day.
2. Put the hardest subject first, in short blocks.
3. Leave the day before each exam for review.

## Pitfalls
- Don’t plan more hours than the user has.

## Verification
Every exam has study blocks before its date.

              Scheduled jobs
              Hermes can do a job on a schedule, without anyone asking.
              
  
              
    
              What’s a cron job?
              
      
              An alarm clock for your agent. At the set time, Hermes wakes the agent up, gives it a task, and sends the result where you want, like Telegram.
              
      
              The schedule is five parts:
              
      
              
        
              0minute
              8hour
              *day
              *month
              *weekday
              
      
              
      
              0 8 * * * means every day at 8:00. A * means “every”.

              Read before you start
              Policies and disclaimer
              The short version of the rules for today.
              
  
              
    
              Workshop disclaimer
              An educational workshop. Examples and sample data are made up. AI output can be wrong or unsafe, so verify before you use it. The SOUL.md tester and Real agents send your messages to the workshop’s Hermes server and the AI model behind it, so never type personal data. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Nous Research or any AI company.
              
    
              Age and accounts
              Each AI tool sets its own minimum age and terms, and some require users to be 18 or older. If you’re under a tool’s age limit, use a different tool, pair up, or follow the projector.
              
    
              Your data
              Lab work stays on this PC. If you pair Telegram, only your pairing code and the username you type are saved. Survey answers go only to the workshop speaker, for attendance and improvement, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Never paste real personal data into AI tools.
              
    
              Academic integrity
              Use AI to learn and build, not to pass off its work as yours. Follow your teachers’ rules on AI use.
              
    
              Responsible use
              The safety examples in this lab are only for protecting your own agent. Never use agents to deceive, spam, collect personal data or get around security.
              
    
              AI tool policies
              Every tool you use today has its own rules. Skim them once.
